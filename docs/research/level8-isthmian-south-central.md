2. **Southall** plays at **SkyEx Community Stadium** in Hayes — a groundshare with Hayes & Yeading United. Same address and postcode (UB4 0SL area) as Hayes & Yeading, though Southall's registered town is Stanwell (TW19 7LD for their training base).

## Coordinate Sources

- **Latitude/Longitude:** Extracted from the Wikipedia `2025–26 Isthmian League` page map template `{{location map~}}` entries for the South Central Division. These are the ground coordinates used on the Wikipedia location maps.
- **Postcodes:** Retrieved via [postcodes.io](https://postcodes.io) reverse geocoding API from the Wikipedia coordinates. Where the default 100m radius returned no result, a wider search (5000m) was used to find the nearest postcode.

## Data Quality Notes

- Coordinates are from the Wikipedia map templates and should be treated as approximate (within ~50m of the actual ground centre).
- Postcodes were derived computationally from coordinates and may not exactly match the ground's postal address. They should be verified against club/official sources before use in production.
- Ground capacity data is available in the Wikipedia table but is not included in this extract (focused on location data).
- The 2025–26 season page was available and used directly (no fallback to 2024–25 was needed).
- Two clubs are in groundshare arrangements (Kingstonian, Southall) — the coordinates reflect the ground they play at, not their historical/administrative home.
- The season is complete — all data reflects final standings and confirmed club allocations.
