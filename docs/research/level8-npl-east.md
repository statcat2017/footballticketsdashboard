## Data Quality Notes

### Coordinates
- All coordinates sourced from the `Coordinates:` line at the top of each club's Wikipedia page (infobox). These typically reflect the ground location.
- **Lincoln United** — the Wikipedia page for Lincoln United does not include coordinate data. The coordinates listed are approximate (Lincoln city area) and should be verified.
- Precision varies: most coordinates are to 6 decimal places; some Wikipedia pages give more or fewer significant figures.

### Postcodes
- Postcodes marked with a postcodes.io source were confirmed via the free API (`api.postcodes.io`), which reverse-geocodes from coordinates. Not all coordinates produce a valid postcode result.
- Postcodes without an API source are based on the known postal district of the ground's location and should be considered estimates pending manual verification.

### Ground Names
- **Dunston**: listed as "Wellington Road" in the NPL table; the ground is now known as the UTS Stadium. Wikipedia page uses "Wellington Road / UTS Stadium".
- **North Ferriby**: listed as "The Dransfield Stadium" in the NPL table; current sponsored name is "Versa Sportswear Stadium".
- **Pontefract Collieries**: listed as "Harratt Nissan Stadium" in the NPL table; also known as "Beechnut Lane".
- **Silsden**: listed as "Keighley Road Stadium" in the NPL table; current sponsored name is "Regal Interiors Stadium". Previously played at Cougar Park (Keighley Cougars RLFC groundshare) prior to 2010.

### Groundshares
No current groundshares identified for the 2025-26 season. Notable historical groundshares:
- **Silsden** played at Cougar Park (Keighley Cougars) prior to 2010.
- **Bishop Auckland** has a history of groundshares (Shildon, West Auckland, Spennymoor) but returned to its own Heritage Park ground in 2010.
- **Pontefract Collieries** had a brief groundshare with Ossett United between September and November 2025 while their new 3G pitch was installed — this was temporary and not a permanent arrangement.
