## Data Quality Notes

- All 22 clubs confirmed from the "Stadia and locations" table of the 2025–26 Isthmian League North Division section.
- Postcodes were obtained via reverse geocoding from club Wikipedia coordinates. Some coordinates initially returned null from postcodes.io due to slight coordinate drift; adjusted coordinates were used where needed and are noted.
- Two groundshares are active this season: Gorleston (at Great Yarmouth Town's Wellesley Recreation Ground) and Grays Athletic (at Tilbury's Chadfields).
- Cambridge City play in Sawston (West Way), not Cambridge city centre, following their 2016 relocation.
- Redbridge play in Barkingside (Oakside Stadium), within the London Borough of Redbridge.
- Coordinates are rounded to 6 decimal places in the table; full-precision values from the Wikipedia API are preserved in the source column.

## Notes

- **Gorleston (Wellesley Recreation Ground):** Groundshare with Great Yarmouth Town (Eastern Counties League). The ground is located in Great Yarmouth, not Gorleston-on-Sea.
- **Grays Athletic (Chadfields):** Grays Athletic groundshare with Tilbury at Chadfields. Grays have been groundsharing since leaving their former New Recreation Ground in 2010.
- **Cambridge City (West Way):** Cambridge City moved to West Way in Sawston in 2016 after leaving the City Ground. Sawston is ~5 miles south of Cambridge city centre.
- **Redbridge (Oakside Stadium):** Also known as the Oakside Stadium, located in Barkingside in the London Borough of Redbridge.
- **Walthamstow (Wadham Lodge):** Wadham Lodge is located in the Walthamstow area of the London Borough of Waltham Forest.
