## Data Quality Notes

### Corrections from existing approximate data
Several existing venue records had significantly inaccurate coordinates:
- **Coleshill Town (Pack Meadow)**: Existing coords pointed to near Watford (51.648, -0.633); corrected to Coleshill, Warwickshire (52.483, -1.698).
- **Coventry Sphinx (Sphinx Drive)**: Existing coords were ~3km west; corrected to actual ground location.
- **Rugby Borough (Kilsby Lane)**: Existing coords were ~4km off; corrected.
- **Basford United (Greenwich Avenue)**: Existing coords were near Bulwell; corrected to Basford area.
- **Rugby Town (Butlin Road)**: Existing coords off by ~2km; corrected.
- **Loughborough Students**: Existing coords were ~2.5km east; corrected to Loughborough University Stadium.
- **Racing Club Warwick**: Existing coords off by ~1.5km; corrected.
- **Boldmere St Michaels**: Existing coords were incorrectly close to Sutton Coldfield Town's Coles Lane; corrected.

### Coordinate Accuracy
- Coordinates from Wikipedia `prop=coordinates` are typically ground-level and accurate to ~6 decimal places (sub-10m precision).
- AFC Rushden & Diamonds and Loughborough Students use Nominatim results (road/building level), marked as approximate.

### Postcodes
- Obtained via postcodes.io reverse geocoding with 500m search radius.
- Most should be accurate to the ground's postal address.

### Groundshares
No groundshares identified among these 22 clubs for the 2025–26 season.
