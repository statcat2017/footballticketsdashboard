## Notes

### Season
- The 2025–26 season was used (a completed season as of May 2026). The Wikipedia article contains the full season data including final league table, results, and stadia.

### Groundshares
- **Kettering Town:** Play at Latimer Park, which they share with Burton Park Wanderers. Explicitly noted on the Wikipedia page.
- **Worcester City:** Play at Sixways Stadium, the home of Worcester Warriors Rugby Football Club. Worcester City have been without a permanent home ground since selling their previous ground in 2013.

### Coordinate Accuracy
- Club-page coordinates from Wikipedia typically reflect the location of the club/ground (most non-league club pages on Wikipedia have coordinates set to the ground's location).
- Ground-specific Wikipedia pages were used where available: Barwell (Kirkby Road), Quorn (Farley Way Stadium), Stourbridge (War Memorial Athletic Ground), Worcester City (Sixways Stadium).
- Coordinates are accurate to approximately 6 decimal places (sub-10m precision).

### Postcodes
- Postcodes were obtained via reverse geocoding through postcodes.io using the latitude/longitude from Wikipedia. This returns the nearest registered postcode to the coordinate point.
- **AFC Sudbury (King's Marsh, Sudbury):** CO10 7GB is the nearest postcode (~500m from the club-page coordinate). King's Marsh is a large sports ground complex on the outskirts of Sudbury; the exact postcode may vary by entrance. Marked as approximate.
