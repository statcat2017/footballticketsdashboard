## Data Quality Notes

- **Coordinates** obtained from Wikipedia API (`prop=coordinates`) or, where unavailable, from Nominatim (OSM) reverse geocoding of the stadium name + town.
- **Postcodes** obtained from Nominatim reverse geocode on stadium coordinates. Where Nominatim had no postcode, `postcodes.io` reverse geocoding was used as fallback.
- **Groundshares** noted in the Wikipedia stadium description and verified.
- Coordinates are in WGS84 (EPSG:4326), rounded to 5 decimal places.
- Postcodes are best-effort from OSM address data — may represent the postal address of the ground rather than the exact pitch location.
