Status: superseded
Owner: Frontend
Priority: medium
Depends on:
Superseded by: TICKET-035

## Purpose

Originally scoped as a lightweight SVG visualiser of the English pyramid using TypeScript constants (14 divisions, 20 edges, Level 8 gap). After PR #77 the model grew to 52 divisions and 129 edges across Levels 1–10 with no structural gaps. TICKET-035 subsumes this ticket with a database-backed interactive explorer and admin edge editor — the scope here is too narrow to be useful on its own.
